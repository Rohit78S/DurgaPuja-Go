Security & Safety

Everything is safe – this site uses HTTPS, official Google Login, and no payment data is stored.

  • HTTPS by default: All traffic is encrypted (Let's Encrypt SSL)
  • Official Google OAuth: If you choose to sign in, it's via Google's secure OAuth flow – we never ask for Google passwords directly
  • Optional Guest mode: You can browse without logging in; Guest users cannot save personal data
  • No sensitive data stored: We do not collect or store banking details, OTPs, or other sensitive personal information
  • Email usage is minimal: Email is used only for confirmations or saving preferences (you can opt out)
  • Security headers & CSP: We set Content Security Policy and other headers to reduce risk of script injections
